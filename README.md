# web-racer

**A street-racing game for a young kid who already beat [rail-shooter](../rail-shooter): race three rivals through a dusk city and a desert canyon in cars covered in spider webs. No crashes, no losing, no reading.**

The twin of rail-shooter, a step less "kiddo". The cars are faster and darker, there are rivals, traffic and nitro, and the music is driving minor-key synth. It's still wordless and still has no fail state.

Every car, building, mesa, icon, sound and music loop is generated by Python scripts. The geometry is **build123d** (OpenCascade CAD) and the audio is synthesised with NumPy. The repo contains no hand-made or third-party art or audio.

## What came from the first playtest
| Seen while he played rail-shooter | Became |
|---|---|
| Leans his whole body when steering | Curves push the car outward (gently), so he steers *into* turns and leans |
| Avoids enemies instead of shooting | **Traffic to dodge**. Passing a car close without touching it refills nitro with a *swish* |
| Laughs spamming the horn | The horn **does something**: traffic ahead moves over a lane with hazards flashing |
| Shoots with intention (hold, not toggle) | **Nitro** is a hold button with a tank that drains and refills |
| "No prob dad, I'll resolve that problem" (moving door) | **Railway crossing** (the arms take turns) and **sliding barriers**. There's always an open side |
| Noticed the birds | Birds, lit city windows, harbour cranes, crowds in the grandstands, a tunnel |
| Excited to finish a level | Fanfare, fireworks, camera swing, then a **flat podium**: all four cars side by side, no places |
| Got the hang of it after 3 levels | Starts at the back of the grid, rivals rubber-band so the race stays close |

## Made for a young kid
- **No fail state.** Walls make you scrape, cars bump, closed gates stop you until they open. Nothing ends the race. If he lets go of everything, the car keeps driving.
- **No places.** The top bar shows every racer's progress. The finish is a flat podium with everyone level.
- **No text.** Icons only. The grown-up menu is hidden behind a 3-second hold.
- **Comfort:** the horizon never rolls, there's no screen shake, and the only FOV change is a gentle widening on nitro.
- **Invisible help.** If he keeps bumping into things, the curve push softens and leading rivals ease off. It never gets harder.
- **Button-mash proof.** Any unbound button honks.

## Play
| | Keyboard | Gamepad |
|---|---|---|
| Steer | A D / ← → | left stick / d-pad |
| Nitro (hold) | Space or Shift | A / Cross, R1 |
| Brake | S / ↓ | stick down |
| Horn | H, or any other key | any other button |
| Pause | Esc | Start |
| Restart race | hold R | pause menu ↻ |
| Grown-up menu | **hold F1 for 3 s**, or hold the ⚙ on the title | **hold Start + Select for 3 s** |

Title screen: ◀ ▶ picks one of 12 cars (2 bodies × 6 web liveries), ▶ goes to track select. Holding nitro through the green light gives a rocket start.

## How it's made
```
tools/*.py ──► assets/cars/*.glb, assets/tracks/*.glb, tracks/*.json, assets/audio/*.wav, assets/ui/*.png ──► Godot 4 ──► .exe
```
- **Cars (build123d):** the body is a side profile ∩ top outline (visual hull), filleted. It has a tapered greenhouse with a painted roof, and wheels sit in boolean-cut arches.
- **Web liveries:** a 2D web (spokes plus sagging threads) is extruded through the car and intersected with a thin skin just outside the paint, so every strand hugs the bodywork. Webs go on the hood, roof, doors, trunk and rear panel, since the chase camera mostly shows the back.
- **Tracks are data:** a closed Catmull-Rom spline is resampled every 2 m into the road ribbon, lane dashes, curbs, walls and embankment. Props are placed only where they clear every stretch of road, and everything is merged into 400 m chunks.
- **Racing model:** each car is *(distance along the track, lateral offset, speed)*. Collisions, near misses, drafting and gates are all analytic in track space, so physics can never trap a car.
- **Audio:** an engine loop with a fake gearbox (pitch climbs, then drops at each shift), a two-tone horn, whooshes, a crossing bell, a fanfare, and minor-key driving loops at 124–128 bpm.
- **Checks:** triangle budgets, minimum curve radius, loop closure, audio peak and seam. Good and bad bots race every track using input actions only.

## Build from source
Requirements: [Godot 4.7.2](https://godotengine.org/download) (as `godot` on PATH), Python 3.10+.
```bash
pip install build123d trimesh numpy scipy pillow
python3 tools/build_all.py            # everything (~2.5 min; the web booleans are the slow part)
python3 tools/build_all.py --no-cars  # tracks, audio, icons only (~45 s)
tools/run_tests.sh                    # build, import, compile check, self-test, bots on every track
godot --path .                        # play from source
godot --headless --export-release "Windows" build/windows/web-racer.exe
```
Flags (after `--`): `--level=N` starts a track directly, `--car=N` picks a car (0–11), `--bot=good|bad` turns on autopilot, `--laps=N`, `--shots` takes a screenshot tour, `--podium` shoots the flat podium, `--check` compile-checks the scripts, `--test` runs the input/save/pause self-test.

Screenshots need a display. Run `sudo apt install xvfb` and `run_tests.sh` takes them on an invisible one.

### Add a track
In `tools/tracks.py`, add a `track_N()` that returns spline control points, heights, scenery, and `gates`/`slides`/`birds`/`traffic`. Add it to `DEFS`, give it a palette in `tools/palette.py`, and add a title icon slot in `scripts/ui/title.gd`.

# Decisions

Format: reading taken — reason — how to change.

## Setup (2026-10-18)
- **New sibling project**, `~/web-racer`. rail-shooter is untouched. Files copied from it (and then adapted): input bindings, save, audio bus, scene flow, icon menu, parent menu, audio helpers, palette ramps/bake, icon renderer, MIT licence, export presets.
- **Same engine and pins:** Godot 4.7.2, Compatibility renderer, unshaded vertex colour, retro 640×360 on by default (parent menu toggles it).
- **Geometry: build123d 0.13** (OpenCascade) for cars and props, as asked. The **road ribbon is numpy** (a 2 km sweep through OCC would be slow and tessellate badly), and so are the embankment and tunnel arch. build123d solids are tessellated to trimesh and flat-shaded by the same palette bake as rail-shooter.
- **Web skin:** `offset_3d` fails on the filleted car hulls, so the skin the webs live in is a per-axis scaled copy of the body (`tools/web.py`, marked `shortcut:`).
- **No xvfb on this machine** (installing it needs sudo). Bots and checks run `--headless`. Screenshot runs open a Godot window on WSLg for ~20 s, so they are only run with the user's OK; `run_tests.sh` uses `xvfb-run` automatically once installed.

## User answers (2026-10-18)
- **Rivals:** "flat podium, just a bar with all players present". Read as: there are rivals and a race, but **no places are ever shown**. The HUD is one bar with every racer on it, and the finish lines all four cars up side by side at the same height. Rivals rubber-band (±12 %) so they stay in sight.
- **Steering:** assisted. Throttle is automatic, left/right steers, down brakes, nitro is a hold button. The car can't spin or turn round.
- **Scope:** vertical slice, 2 tracks. No sticker book or unlocks: all 12 cars are pickable from the start.

## Gameplay readings
- **Curve push** = curvature × v² × 0.15 m/s outward (about 3 m/s on the tightest curve at cruise). It's there to make him lean into turns. The assist scales it down by up to 60 % after repeated bumps.
- **Near miss** = overtaking traffic with less than 1.6 m between the cars while going 4 m/s faster: +20 % nitro and a swish. **Drafting** behind a rival fills 12 %/s. A slow passive refill (3 %/s) means the tank is never empty for long.
- **Horn:** traffic within 80 m ahead and 3.5 m sideways moves one lane away from you, hazards flashing. The bot honks every 4 s to exercise this.
- **Gates:** the railway crossing's two arms take turns (one half of the road is always open), and the bell rings when they swap. A car can't drive into a closed arm and waits until it lifts. Traffic queues politely. Sliding barriers sweep ±5.5 m across the 18 m road and always leave a side open. Rivals and the bot predict where the barrier will be when they arrive.
- **Traffic recycling:** traffic more than 120 m behind the player reappears 260–480 m ahead in a lane with room, so traffic density follows the player.
- **Start:** the player is last on the grid (overtaking is the fun). Holding nitro through GO gives a rocket start.
- **Car sizes are analytic** (2.0 × 4.6 m boxes in track space). Traffic is heavier in collisions, so racers bounce off it.

## Rename + 4 tracks + car unlocks (user, 2026-10-19)
- **Name: curious-for-speed** (repo github.com/MarAvFe/curious-for-speed). The local folder stays `~/web-racer` so the open workspace keeps working. Godot derives the save folder from the name, so saves now live under `curious-for-speed/`; no family save existed under the old name.
- **Tracks 3–4:** a second city (Neon Downtown, a figure-8 with an overpass) and a non-city track (Frost Pass, snow and a frozen lake). Still no oncoming traffic.
- **8 cars** (2 bodies × 4 liveries), all shown in one grid on the title. Locked cars are black silhouettes and can't be picked. **Every finished race unlocks the next car**, even when he repeats the same track. Grid colour order is orange, black, red, green, as the user asked. The unlock order alternates bodies within each colour (orange stinger, orange brute, black stinger, …), so the second body appears on the first win. Blue and purple stay rivals-only for variety. The newly won car is revealed on the title with a sparkle and the fanfare.
- **Save fix:** JSON loads numbers as floats, and the type-checked merge used to reject them for integer fields, so the chosen car was never restored. Integer fields now accept floats.
- **Overpasses:** elevated samples get no embankment, a deck underside and fascia, and pillars that stop under the deck and are skipped where they would stand on another stretch of road. Gates and barriers are placed with `flat_spot()`: on the ground and at least 70 m from any other stretch of road.
- **Faceted props:** OCC caches a shape's first triangulation, and lofts produce B-spline faces that tessellate finely. So pines use plain cones tessellated coarsely (fresh shape per call). This took them from about 1,300 to 540 triangles.
- **Release:** pushed over SSH with a `v0.1.0` tag. The `gh` CLI isn't installed, so the user creates the release from the tag on the web and uploads the binaries.

## Split screen + playable vehicles + colour picker (user, 2026-10-26)
- **Up to 4 players, split screen.** Solo is unchanged and renders straight to the screen. With 2 players the views are stacked top/bottom; with 3–4 they are quarters, and with 3 the spare quarter follows a rival. Each view is a `SubViewport` sharing the one world and has its own HUD (its own nitro tank, and its own dot drawn biggest on the bar). The podium switches back to one full-screen view.
- **Humans take the back of the grid; rivals fill the rest** (4 cars total, so 4 players race with no rivals). The rubber band now keys on the **human furthest back who is still racing**, so the kid is never left alone at the back by a faster parent. Traffic recycles once it's behind *every* player and reappears ahead of a random one. Near misses, drafting (now behind any racer, not just rivals), the horn and gate bells are per player. The race ends when every player has crossed the line; earlier finishers cruise on with their camera swung round.
- **Controls: 4 keys per player (user, same day, replacing pad-per-player joining).** P1 A D W S, P2 arrows, P3 J L I K, P4 V N G B, in the order left, right, up, down. Sides steer, **up = horn, down = nitro (hold)**. The user didn't assign down; nitro is the core fun, and the throttle is automatic, so **brake was dropped** (also from learn mode). Pads get mapped onto these keys outside the game, so in multiplayer the per-player actions (`action@k`, `Bindings.act`) hold only that player's key and never raw pad events (a mapped pad would otherwise drive player 1 as well). Solo, player 1's actions also carry every pad, the learned bindings and the old extras (arrows, Space, H), so the kid's existing setup keeps working. Godot's `InputEvent.new()` defaults to device 0, so only the first pad used to work; base actions now listen on every pad (`device = -1`).
- **Garage flow:** player count first (1–4 people icons + ⚙), then shapes, then colours. Each player moves only their own cursor; **Enter is global** and moves everyone on together. There's no per-player "ready", as asked. Esc steps back. Locked picks shake on Enter. Each player's 4 keys are drawn as keycaps above their turntable (arrow keys as drawn triangles, in case the font lacks arrow glyphs), and an Enter keycap pulses at the bottom. On the grid, up moves the cursor, so the horn is muted there. Returning from a race goes straight back to the grid with the same players.
- **Vehicles:** every shape is playable: wedge, muscle, and the traffic **hatch, pick-up, van**, plus three new ones, **bubble car, jeep, monster truck**. That's 8, so the unlock count is unchanged: an old save's "N cars unlocked" now means "N vehicles". Tall bodies get a `lift` so door/rear webs and tail lights move up with the paint. Traffic keeps its own plain, web-less models.
- **Colours are free, shapes unlock.** After the shapes, each player's sides pick one of 6 liveries (blue and purple are no longer rivals-only). Instead of baking 8×6 = 48 GLBs (~37 MB), each shape is baked once in livery 0 and `Game.car()` swaps exact vertex colours (paint ramp, web ramp, their outline colours). `tools/cars.py` asserts that those colours are unique on the car, and the self-test asserts that a recolour actually changes the mesh, which catches an importer that starts converting colours.
- **Not saved:** players 2–4's picks live for the session only. Player 1's vehicle and colour are saved.
- **Unlocks with friends (user):** with 3–4 players every vehicle is open in the garage for that session, and the saved progress is untouched. Choosing 2 players raises the saved unlock count to at least 2, for good, and each win then reveals the next vehicle as usual.
